The consultation

Every plan starts with listening

Before any treatment, you’ll sit down with a provider to talk through what you’d like to change, and what you’d rather keep. Some people leave with a plan; some decide to wait. Both are good outcomes.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Your goals, concerns and questions, in your own words.

  2. 02

    Assess

    A private review of relevant health history and an in-person assessment.

  3. 03

    Advise

    Honest options, including risks, recovery and alternatives.

  4. 04

    Decide

    Take your time. There is no obligation to book a treatment.

FAQs

Questions, answered gently

General information only. It isn’t medical advice. Your provider will answer questions about your own situation at consultation.

What happens at a consultation?

You’ll talk through your goals and any concerns, and a provider will review relevant health history and assess whether a treatment may be appropriate. You can ask about options, risks, recovery and alternatives. There is no obligation to proceed.

How do I know which treatment is right for me?

That’s exactly what the consultation is for. Treatments that work well for one person aren’t always suitable for another, so recommendations are made individually after an assessment, not online.

Will my results look natural?

Pure MedSpa’s approach favors subtle, natural-looking changes. That said, every person responds differently and no outcome can be guaranteed. Your provider will talk through realistic expectations before you decide.

Are there side effects or downtime?

All aesthetic treatments carry some risk, and recovery varies by treatment and by person. Your provider will explain the possible side effects, aftercare and what to watch for, specific to the treatment you’re considering.

How much do treatments cost?

Pricing depends on the treatment and on your individual plan. Current pricing will be confirmed by the clinic; please ask during your consultation or send an enquiry.

Should I share medical details in the enquiry form?

Please don’t. The enquiry form is for contact details and general questions only. Health information is best discussed privately with your provider at consultation.
